My reading — inference, not cited

LQSS Part IV fixes the test at time of use, not time of batch start. Analyses run after the expiry timestamp therefore sit outside it; analyses completed before it do not.

OCM does not address mid-run expiry anywhere in the corpus. The conservative reading is to re-run only the affected injections, not the whole batch — but that is my inference from the “time of use” wording, not a rule OCM states.
